DEAD HORIZON 1879

BLACKRIDGE, JOKYLAND➜ CHAPTER 1: THE MAN WHO RAN
SAMUEL "SAM" CALLOWAY

Thirty-six years old. Once a dangerous outlaw — trains robbed, horses stolen, gangs ridden with, people betrayed. Sam has been gone from Jokyland a long time. He's come back wanting nothing more than a quiet life.

BLACKRIDGE

His past doesn't stay buried. People in Blackridge recognize his face. Old debts don't forgive themselves, and a man named Victor Kane — someone Sam once rode with — believes Sam betrayed every one of them.

THE QUESTION

Sam's goal was never just to survive out here. It's to find out whether a violent man can actually become something better. Every shot you do or don't take in this chapter starts answering that.

This build covers Chapter 1's opening beats — arriving in Blackridge, an old debt, and the trouble that follows. Honor and a wanted level track your choices, and there's a horse in the stable if you'd rather ride than walk.
